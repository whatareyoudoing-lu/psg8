📜 How to Play

1. Secret Poison Setup:

Player 1 secretly selects 1 card as their poison (Player 2 looks away). Then, Player 2 secretly selects 1 card as their poison (Player 1 looks away).

2. The Duel & Meaning Challenge:

Take turns picking a card. Before clicking to flip, say its English meaning out loud based on the Chinese & Pinyin on the front! Click to reveal the English back.

3. Shatter & Game Over:

If anyone flips either poison card, a bottle shatter sound plays, the screen shakes, and that player gets poisoned and loses immediately!
